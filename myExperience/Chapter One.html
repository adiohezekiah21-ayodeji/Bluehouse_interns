CHAPTER ONE

Agriculture is the science, practice and business of cultivating land, raising crops, and rearing animals for food, fiber, fuel and other products human use. It is the delibrate management of plants and animals to produce what we need to live. Instead of hunting/gathering , agriculture involves planting, watering, weeding, feeding, breeding and harvesting.

Crop Farming

We shall be discussing Crop Farming under two sub-sections basically: Definition and Types

Definition of Crop Farming

Crop farming is the branch of agriculture focused on growing and harvesting plants for food, fiber, fuel or other economic use.

What does crop farming invloves:

  • Preparing soil, planting seeds/seedlings
  • Managing water, nutrients, pests and weeds
  • Harvesting mature plants at the right time
  • Storing and preparing the yield for sale or use

Types of Crops

  • Food crops: Maize, rice, wheat, cassave, vegetables, fruits
  • Cash crops: Grown for sale rather than consumption - cocoa, cotton, cofee, palm oil
  • Forage crops: Grown to feed livestock - alfalfa, clover
  • Industrial crops: Used for fuel, fiber or processing - sugarcane, rubber, tobacco

Animal Husbandry

We shall be discussing Animal Husbandry too under two sub-sections basically: Definition and Types

Definition of Animal Husbandry

Animal Husbandry is the branch of agriculture that deals with breeding, raising, and caring for livestock and other animals for food, fiber, labour and other products

What does animal husbandry covers:

  • Breeding: Selecting and mating animals to improve traits like growth rate, milk yield, disease resistance
  • Feeding and nutrition: Providing balanced feed and water
  • Housing & Health: Building shelters, preventing disease, veterinary care.
  • Management: Handling reproduction, weaning, record-keeping, marketing

Common animals involved

  • Livestock: Cattle, goats, sheep, pigs for meat, milk, hides
  • Poultry: Chickens, ducks, turkeys for meat and eggs
  • Aquaculture: Fish, shrimp, mollusks for food
  • Beekeeping: Honeybees for honey, wax, pollination
